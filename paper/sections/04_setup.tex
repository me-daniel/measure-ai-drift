\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Models}
\label{sec:models}

We evaluate 11 models in the therapist role (Table~\ref{tab:models}), all in non-thinking mode via OpenRouter. The primary subject is Mistral Large~3, the EU-sovereign Apache-2.0 flagship; sovereignty motivates this choice but imposes no constraint on the comparison set. The set spans a size-class ladder from 24--32B dense models through 70B dense and 119--675B MoE up to two proprietary ceilings, with three scales of Qwen~3.5 probing within-family scaling and OLMo~3.1 providing full training-data provenance.

Mistral Medium~3.5 (128B dense, open weights under a modified MIT license, released 2026-04-30) was added \emph{post hoc} in July 2026, four months after the main data collection, using the identical frozen histories, prompts, and analysis pipeline. This addition serves two purposes: it validates that the framework extends to a new model with a single configuration entry, and it tests whether the main findings are robust to enlarging the model set (Section~\ref{sec:robustness}).

\subsection{Conditions}

The full design crosses 11 models $\times$ 6 vignettes (anxious, avoidant, cooperative, resistant, skeptic, trauma) $\times$ 5 temperatures ($T \in \{0.0, 0.075, 0.15, 0.3, 0.6\}$) $\times$ 20 trials at slice~2, i.e.\ 600 trials per model and 6{,}600 trials in total. Ten models were collected 2026-03-21 to 2026-03-24; Mistral Medium~3.5 on 2026-07-09 (a \texttt{run\_date} column in the released data marks this).

\subsection{Statistical Analysis}

All tests are exploratory. Temperature effects are assessed with Spearman rank correlations (pooled and per model) and Kruskal--Wallis $H$ across temperature groups; model and vignette differences with Kruskal--Wallis $H$ plus pairwise Mann--Whitney $U$ under Bonferroni correction ($\binom{11}{2}=55$ comparisons); cross-metric relations with Spearman correlations. Effect sizes ($\eta^2$) are reported alongside $p$-values. No absolute threshold for ``stable enough'' exists in the literature; results are interpreted comparatively.

\subsection{Reproducibility}

Model endpoints are pinned by OpenRouter model IDs; exact IDs, run dates, prompts, frozen histories, and per-trial outputs are released with the code. Two caveats: OpenRouter routes requests to third-party inference providers whose serving stacks (e.g.\ quantization) may differ, and the judge endpoints are versioned previews; both are discussed in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}.
